Boundary & Method

All traces are abstracted, public-safe samples. No original conversations, titles, or private vocabularies appear here; each cloud was confirmed by the person whose materials they distill.

Words pressed under red marks, such as continue, okay, and search, are surface wear left by platform mechanisms. They do not take part in the interpretation. The developing process stops before completion: some relations remain unsettled.

All developing happens only on this screen. Leave or refresh, and the paper returns to its initial state.
Method and misreading risk: Runaway Checkpoint (Runo). This note is the only place where the author is allowed to appear inside the work.

展签：手痕词云：共塑显影

Runaway Checkpoint / 鹿诺 · 交互网页作品 · 2026

《手痕词云：共塑显影》不是一张用户画像，也不是一次对私人对话的展示。它把长期 AI 共作中反复出现的判断、边界、材料、制度和情感词痕抽象成六团公开安全的云。观众点击一朵云，词痕先显影，随后平台机制的表面磨损被红线压回纸面，人与 AI 之间的互相塑形关系才逐渐浮出。

这件作品关心的不是“AI 读懂了谁”，而是长期共作怎样改变双方的提问、标准和行动方式。词云里的每条回流线都提醒观众：一次判断从来不只停在一个主题里，它会流向历史尺度、制度设计、亲密关系、身体叙事和技术政治。

公开边界：作品不包含对话原文、标题、私人词表、日期节奏或可反向识别细节。所有显影只发生在本次观看中，刷新即复原。

Label: Handtrace Word Cloud — Co-Shaping, Developing

Runaway Checkpoint / Runo · interactive web artwork · 2026

Handtrace Word Cloud — Co-Shaping, Developing is not a user portrait, and it does not display private conversations. It abstracts recurring traces of judgment, boundary-making, material, institution, and intimacy from a long human-AI collaboration into six public-safe clouds. When viewers click a cloud, word traces appear first; then surface wear from platform mechanisms is pressed back onto the paper by red marks, and the mutual shaping between human and AI begins to emerge.

The work is not about whether an AI has “understood” someone. It asks how long collaboration changes the questions, standards, and actions of both sides. Each return line between clouds reminds viewers that a judgment never remains inside one topic: it flows toward historical scale, institutional design, intimacy, embodied narrative, and techno-politics.

Boundary: the work contains no original conversations, titles, private vocabularies, date rhythms, or reversible identifying details. All developing happens only during the current viewing session; refresh the page and the paper returns to its initial state.
